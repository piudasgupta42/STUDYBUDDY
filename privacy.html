Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Data Collection

Sakha & Sakhi does not require user accounts. Messages typed into the chat may be temporarily processed through third-party AI APIs to generate responses.

2. Storage

The application does not store personal data on external servers. Some temporary data such as selected avatar or session timer may be stored locally in your browser using localStorage.

3. Third-Party Services

AI responses may be generated using free-tier AI providers such as HuggingFace. Please review their privacy policies separately.

4. Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or analytics tools.

5. Children’s Privacy

This application is intended for students. It does not knowingly collect personal identification information.

6. Contact

For questions regarding this policy, please contact the developer.
